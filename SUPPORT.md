# Getting help

Default support pointers for Immersive Fusion's open source repositories. Where a
repository publishes its own `SUPPORT.md`, that one wins.

## Where to go

**A bug, or something that looks like one:** open an issue on the repository itself.
That is the right place even if you are not sure it is a bug. Include the version or
commit, what you expected, and what happened instead.

**A question about using one of the tools:** [our Discord](https://discord.gg/zevywnQp6K)
is usually faster than an issue, and other people can see the answer.

**A security issue:** do not open an issue. See `SECURITY.md`, or mail
security@immersivefusion.com.

**Product documentation:** [docs.deepcube.ai](https://docs.deepcube.ai).

## What we can and cannot promise

These are open source projects maintained by a small team. Issues get read. Not all of
them get fixed, and some get closed with a reason rather than a patch. We would rather
tell you that plainly than leave an issue open for a year.

Pull requests are welcome on every one of these repositories. If you are thinking of
building something substantial, open an issue first and check the direction, so nobody
spends a weekend on a change we cannot merge.

## If you are reporting something on behalf of a company

You do not need permission and you do not need an account with us. These tools are
Apache-2.0 and you are welcome to use them without ever talking to us.
