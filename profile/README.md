## Enter the World of Your Application®

**Immersive Fusion** builds **spatial observability**. Our product is **DeepCube**: your live system rendered as a place you can walk into, so a fault is somewhere you go, not something you reassemble in your head at 3 a.m. Continuity, proximity, and memory instead of forty-seven tabs, so you find it faster. Human on the loop.

### The three tests

Spatial observability is not a dashboard with a 3D skin. Here is how you tell the difference. Each test names a place the fog used to cost you time.

- **Continuity.** The payments service today is the one you saw last Tuesday, wherever the layout draws it. Returning is a comparison, so you notice what changed instead of rebuilding it from scratch.
- **Proximity.** Stand at a node and the blast radius is already in frame: who calls in, what it depends on, where a fault spreads. No query to write, no filter to babysit.
- **Memory.** Your position and what you have ruled out persist across the incident, and the AI partner beside you reads that same context. The thread is never lost.

Remove those three costs and the path from "something is wrong" to "it is fixed" gets shorter. That is the point.

### Absence is the part flat tools cannot draw

A dashboard can only show what is reporting. It cannot draw the service that should be there and isn't. Spatial observability renders that gap as a phantom, lit among the live callers still reaching for it, so absence points at its own cause.

### The partner in the room

Tessa is not a chatbot in a sidebar. She shares your view, because your position scopes it, and she proposes the only way a partner in a place can: by pointing. She can mark anywhere and move nothing. Human on the loop is the load-bearing part, because the one who answers for what happens next is the one who should decide it. It is not the metaverse: no NFTs, no virtual land, it runs on the screen already in front of you.

### Open by default

OpenTelemetry-native, with no proprietary agents to install. Use any OTel-compatible collector, export your data whenever you want, and keep it in the format it arrived in. No query language to learn, nothing locked in.

Open source lives here too: an [OpenTelemetry trace generator](https://github.com/ImmersiveFusion/opentelemetry-tracegen), a [chaos simulator](https://github.com/ImmersiveFusion/opentelemetry-chaos-sim), and an [MCP server](https://github.com/ImmersiveFusion/deepcube-mcp-server) for querying live telemetry from your editor.
